# Example prompts

Start with something read only, then move on to driving a phone.

## Look

- "List my phones and tell me which ones are offline."
- "Take a screenshot of phone 12 and tell me what app is open."
- "Screenshot every phone in the Marketing group and tell me which ones show an error or a pop-up."

## Drive one phone

- "On phone 12, open Settings and tell me which iOS version it runs."
- "On phone 12, open Safari, go to example.com and screenshot the page."
- "On phone 7, close whatever app is open and go back to the home screen."

## Many phones

- "Do the Safari check on phone 12 first. If it works, do the same on phones 13 to 20 and give me a table of the results."

## Who used what

- "Who was on phone 12 yesterday, and for how long?"
