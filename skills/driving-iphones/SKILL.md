# Driving iPhones on ALI Remote

These are real iPhones, not simulators. Every tap lands on a physical screen, and some actions cannot be undone. Work the way a careful person would with a phone in their hand.

## Look before you touch

- Find the phone first by listing the phones on the account. Check it is online and showing its screen before doing anything else.
- Take a screenshot before every decision. Never tap from memory or from what the screen "should" look like.
- Read tap positions straight off the latest screenshot and send them as they are. The screenshot's pixels are the coordinate space, so do not scale them.
- After an action that matters, take another screenshot and confirm it did what you meant. A swipe can be accepted and still move nothing.

## One phone before many

- Do the task end to end on one phone first. Only repeat it on others once it worked there.
- Phones differ: screen sizes, iOS versions, app versions and pop-ups vary. Screenshot each phone instead of reusing positions from another one.

## Be careful with real accounts

- Ask before anything that sends, posts, deletes, buys or signs out. Reading and looking need no permission.
- If a pop-up, login prompt or code request appears that you did not expect, stop and tell the person instead of guessing.

## When something goes wrong

- If a tap lands in the wrong place, take a fresh screenshot and try once more before trying anything else.
- If a call is refused because of rate limits, wait as long as the response says before trying again.
- If a call is refused for missing permission, tell the person which permission it named. Do not look for a way around it.
